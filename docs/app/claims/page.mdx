# Compensation & Claims

SpaceShield implements a transparent, pull-based compensation model to protect operator collateral and ensure fair subscriber distribution.

---

## Pro-Rata Entitlement Formula

Payout amounts depend on the subscriber's active coverage duration relative to the global `COMPENSATION_WINDOW`:

```text
Claimable Amount = (Operator Collateral Bond * Subscriber Active Duration) / COMPENSATION_WINDOW
```

### Example

- **Operator Bond**: 100,000 CTC
- **Subscriber Coverage**: 30 days active out of 30-day window (100%)
- **Payout**: 100,000 CTC (pro-rata allocated among active subscribers)

---

## Pull-Based Payout Security

Subscribers call `claim(settlementId)` on `SettlementContract`. Using pull-based payments instead of push-transfers eliminates:

- Gas limit exhaustion vulnerabilities when distributing to thousands of subscribers.
- Denial of Service (DoS) attacks caused by failing subscriber contract fallback functions.
- Reentrancy vulnerabilities.
